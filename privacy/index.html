Privacy Policy

SEC (Secure Chat) · Last updated 17 November 2026
Developer: Rokas Butnorius · securechatsec.vercel.app

This Privacy Policy explains how SEC (Secure Chat) handles information when you use the desktop or mobile client or connect to the official relay.

1. Summary

  • Message content is end-to-end encrypted. The server cannot read your messages.
  • Private keys never leave your device.
  • The server can see limited metadata (who you message, groups, online status, public keys, and optional profile information).
  • Anonymous mode creates a temporary identity with almost no permanent storage.
  • This is still an early experimental project and has not been independently audited.

2. Information We Process

Stored only on your device

  • Private keys and Double Ratchet session data
  • Encrypted local vault (protected by your passphrase)
  • Optional “remember me” unlock data (uses platform protection such as Windows DPAPI when available)

Processed by the official relay (wss://relay.flashchat.store)

  • User ID you choose
  • Public cryptographic keys needed for encryption
  • Optional profile (bio + avatar)
  • Contacts and group membership
  • Online / offline status
  • Encrypted message envelopes (temporary queue only)
  • Call signalling (forwarded, not stored long-term)

We do not collect message content, private keys, passphrases, real name, email, phone number, or location.

3. How We Use the Information

Only to operate the messaging and calling service: deliver encrypted messages, maintain contacts/groups/presence, and keep the relay working. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

4. End-to-End Encryption

SEC uses X3DH-style key agreement + Double Ratchet with XChaCha20-Poly1305. Voice/video calls (desktop) use WebRTC with DTLS-SRTP. All encryption happens on the user’s device.

5. Data Retention

  • Encrypted messages are deleted from the server after the recipient acknowledges them.
  • Public keys, contacts, groups and profile remain while the account exists.
  • You can delete local data at any time by removing the vault/session files.
  • You can request deletion of server-side data by emailing the contact addresses above.

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • Use Anonymous mode
  • Delete local data yourself
  • Request deletion of your server-side account data
  • Access, correct or object to processing of your personal data (where applicable under GDPR)

7. Children’s Privacy (Lithuania & EU)

Under Lithuanian law implementing GDPR Article 8, the age of digital consent is 14 years.

  • If you are under 14, processing of your personal data for this service normally requires consent from a parent or legal guardian.
  • From age 14 you can generally provide your own consent.
  • SEC is not specifically directed at children under 14. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 14 without appropriate consent, we will delete that data.

Note: Other countries have different ages — for example 13 under US COPPA or 16 in some EU countries. We follow the rules of the user’s country where possible.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use means you accept the updated version.
